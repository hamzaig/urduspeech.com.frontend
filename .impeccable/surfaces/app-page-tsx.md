# Homepage

Mode: Persuade

Audience: Pakistani creators (reels, YouTube, podcasts, interviews) who need captions their audience reads in Urdu script, Roman Urdu, English, or both. Job: believe four things at once — the Urdu comes out right (real Nastaliq, correct names), it is fast and simple, it is built for Pakistani creators (PKR, local payments), and the captions look good on social video. Action: start the 10 free minutes. Proof: the live sample caption demo and illustrative sample media (labelled), no invented users, reviews, or accuracy claims. Constraints: light and dark themes both ship; reference sites Speechify and Kalakar set the craft bar only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A recording is printed into captions the way ajrak is printed onto cloth — layer by layer, block by block. The page refuses the category's black-ground-plus-one-neon split hero.

OWN-WORLD: Ajrak block-print. Light theme is resist-white cotton (cool, never cream) printed in indigo ink with madder-red fields; dark theme is the indigo-dyed cloth with madder and resist-white printing. Madder red owns whole bands (hero, close). Black keylines, square block-grid tiles, border bands of repeating stamped motifs (authored SVG), resist-dot details. Nastaliq is the display voice for Urdu; Bricolage carries Latin.

STORY: The visitor sees one sample frame captioned in four scripts, stamps between them, understands outputs and exports, sees PKR plans, and starts free.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed madder field under a motif border. A two-line Latin headline with a Nastaliq line printed beneath it; primary action "Try 10 free minutes" directly under. Below, centred and large, the sample video frame with the caption printed on it, four block-stamp language controls attached to its lower edge; each press re-stamps the caption.
