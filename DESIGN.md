# Design System: UrduSpeech

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Ajrak Print Table"**

A recording is printed into captions the way ajrak is printed onto cloth: layer by layer, block by block. The whole public site is one bolt of cloth. In light theme the ground is resist-white cotton (cool, hue 255, never cream) printed in indigo ink, with madder red owning whole fields. In dark theme the cloth itself is indigo-dyed, and the same blocks print in madder and resist white. Motifs are authored SVG blocks (a sixteen-point star with keyline ring, resist dots, a lozenge border band) applied as CSS masks, so each one prints in whatever ink its region sets and reprints correctly in both themes.

Density is editorial and generous: wide madder bands at the top and close of the homepage, an indigo workflow band in the middle, and resist-white sections between them divided by stamped border bands and 2px keylines rather than cards. Nastaliq is the display voice for Urdu; Bricolage Grotesque carries Latin; Public Sans does the work. The sample caption frame, captioned in four scripts and re-printed by square block-stamp controls, is the system's signature.

The world refuses the category's black-ground-plus-one-neon split hero. Red is cloth here, not a warning light.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Madder fields as whole bands (hero, close, interior product hero), indigo as ink and as cloth.
- Every motif is a mask-printed SVG block that takes its region's ink.
- 2px keylines frame print blocks; 1px rules divide within them.
- Small, square corners (2 to 8px); tags are dashed printed tickets, not capsules.
- Nastaliq set large, untracked, weight 400, line-height 1.9 to 2.1.
- Motion is a stamp: a line lands slightly large and soft, then settles.

## Colors

Three dyes on one cloth: resist white, indigo ink, madder red, with a near-black keyline for structure.

### Primary
- **Madder Red** (`madder`, dark `dark-madder`): the field dye. Fills the homepage hero and close bands, the interior `.product-hero` panel, the bilingual reading row, the inked (selected) stamp block and `::selection`. Text on it is Madder Ink (`madder-ink`), secondary text Madder Muted (`madder-muted`).
- **Madder Text Red** (`accent`, hover `accent-hover`, dark `dark-accent`): the same hue at text-safe lightness for buttons, inline links, the manifest diamonds, the Urdu promise line, the active nav underline and form accent-color. In dark theme it lifts to a light coral (`dark-accent`) with indigo text on it (`dark-accent-ink`).

### Secondary
- **Indigo Ink** (`indigo`, `print`): the printing ink in light theme. Border bands, the sample-frame body, the workflow band and the text on resist-white CTAs. In dark theme `print` becomes coral (`dark-print`) so bands still read on indigo cloth.
- **Stage Indigo** (`stage`, `stage-deep`, dark `dark-stage`): the footer and media surfaces, with Stage Ink text, Stage Muted secondary text and Stage Coral (`stage-accent`) for the step numerals and footer band.

### Tertiary
- **Keyline Black** (`keyline`): 2px borders around print blocks (hero frame, stamp blocks, reading grid, product hero, CTA) and the border band over a madder hero.

### Neutral
- **Resist White** (`resist` / `paper`): the page ground and the CTA face on madder.
- **Washed Cotton** (`paper-2`, `paper-3`): alternate section grounds (styles, pricing) and secondary hover.
- **Bleached Surface** (`surface`, `surface-hover`): pricing table, reading grid, inputs.
- **Indigo Text** (`ink`, `ink-2`, `muted`): body text ladder, all at hue 266.
- **Thread Rules** (`rule`, `rule-strong`, `control`): 1px dividers, stronger table borders, input strokes.
- **Status** (`error`, `success`, `warn-ink` and dark counterparts): app feedback only, never decoration.

### Named Rules
**The Madder Is Ground Rule.** Madder appears as a field that owns a whole band or block, never as a small tint chip or badge fill. Small red marks use `accent`. The recommended pricing column takes at most a 7% madder mix.

**The Cool Resist Rule.** The light ground stays cool (hue 255, chroma ≤0.013). Warm or cream paper breaks the cloth.

**The Mask-Print Rule.** Motifs exist only as authored SVG masks (`--ajrak-field`, `--ajrak-keys`, `--ajrak-resist`, `--ajrak-band`, `--ajrak-dots`) colored by `background-color` from the region's ink (`--band-ink`, defaulting to `print`). Never bitmap patterns, never SVGs with baked-in fills.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Bricolage Grotesque Variable (Latin), with Public Sans Variable and system-ui fallback
**Body Font:** Public Sans Variable, with system-ui fallback
**Urdu Font:** CaptionUrdu = Noto Nastaliq Urdu, `unicode-range` limited to Arabic script, so Latin inside a CaptionUrdu stack falls through to Public Sans

**Character:** A tight, ink-heavy grotesk for Latin headlines against a flowing Nastaliq line printed beneath it; Public Sans stays quiet and legible underneath both.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (700, `clamp(2.75rem, 5.4vw + 0.5rem, 5.75rem)`, 0.98, -0.038em): the hero headline, max 16ch, centered on madder.
- **Headline** (700, `clamp(2rem, 2.6vw + 0.9rem, 3.4rem)`, 1.02): section heads, max 44rem column. Base `h2` token is `--text-section`.
- **Title** (700, `clamp(1.25rem, 0.75vw + 0.875rem, 1.55rem)`, 1.2): `h3`, step titles, promise terms (at `--text-xl` 1.5625rem).
- **Numeral** (700, `clamp(8rem, 13vw, 14rem)`, 0.78, -0.06em, tabular): workflow step numbers filling their grid block in Stage Coral.
- **Lead** (400, `clamp(1.0625rem, 0.35vw + 0.95rem, 1.25rem)`, 1.5): intro paragraphs, max 46ch.
- **Body** (400, 1rem, 1.55): running text, max about 62ch.
- **Label** (650, 0.8125rem, tabular): tickets, cue times, fact labels. Sentence case; no uppercase eyebrows.
- **Urdu Display** (400, `clamp(1.6rem, 1.6vw + 1rem, 2.4rem)`, 2): the Nastaliq line under the hero headline and in the close.
- **Urdu Caption** (400, `clamp(1.35rem, 2.6vw + 0.5rem, 2.6rem)`, 1.9): the Urdu line printed on the sample frame.
- **Caption Latin** (650, `clamp(1rem, 1.8vw + 0.4rem, 1.75rem)`, 1.35): Roman Urdu and English caption lines, with a soft dark text-shadow.

Type steps (`--text-xs` to `--text-3xl`): 0.8125, 0.875, 1, 1.25, 1.5625, 1.953, 2.441rem.

### Named Rules
**The Nastaliq Never-Tracks Rule.** Urdu is never letter-spaced, never bolded (weight 400) and never set below line-height 1.7 (1.9 to 2.1 for running text). Tracking breaks letter joining.

**The Two-Voice Rule.** Urdu text is always `lang="ur"` in CaptionUrdu; Latin headlines are Bricolage. On `/ur`, everything is Nastaliq right-to-left except Latin values (prices, step numerals) which keep Bricolage, and the sample editor which stays an LTR English interface.

## Layout

A centered `1360px` container with `64px` gutters (`--container`, `--gutter`), sitting inside full-bleed colored bands. The homepage reads as bands: madder hero, resist manifest strip, resist and washed-cotton sections, the indigo workflow band, and a madder close that sits inside the footer. Section padding is `clamp(64px, 7vw, 120px)` (`--home-gap`, 56px under 40rem); the global ladder is `--section-gap-s/--section-gap/--section-gap-l` (120, 144, 176px).

Spacing is a 4px-based scale (2 to 144px). Two-column sections split 5fr / 7fr with a 64px gap; the reading grid is three columns plus a full-width bilingual row; the workflow is three square-ish blocks over a four-column export strip.

Responsive: at `60rem` all split layouts collapse to one column, the reading grid and steps stack, and exports go to two columns. At `40rem` the sample frame changes from 16:9 to 4:3, stamp blocks go two-by-two, and the interior product hero goes edge to edge with no side borders or radius. Hit targets stay at least 44px.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is printed, not lifted. Surfaces sit flat; layering comes from ink over cloth (two mask layers on madder: indigo star blocks at 34% and resist dots at 42%), from keylines, and from band contrast. Shadows are few and soft, tinted with indigo hue 266 (or madder hue 27 on the hero CTA).

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Card** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 2px oklch(20% 0.06 266 / 0.06)`): resting cards in app surfaces.
- **Soft** (`box-shadow: 0 2px 8px oklch(20% 0.06 266 / 0.07)`): menus and raised controls.
- **Lift** (`box-shadow: 0 8px 20px oklch(20% 0.06 266 / 0.12)`): overlays.
- **Field CTA** (`box-shadow: 0 6px 16px oklch(15% 0.06 27 / 0.28)`, hover `0 10px 22px … / 0.32`): only the resist-white CTA on a madder field.

Dark theme deepens the three base shadows to 0.45, 0.48 and 0.62 alpha at oklch 6%.

### Named Rules
**The Calm Zone Rule.** Print layers fade to plain madder behind running text (a radial calm zone behind hero and close copy). Resist dots must never sit behind words; on `/ur` they read as Nastaliq nuqtas.

## Shapes

Square block geometry. Corners run from 2px (`strip`: tickets, stamp blocks, cue buttons, plan badges) through 5px (`control`: buttons, inputs, nav links, brand mark) and 6px (`card`: reading grid, pricing) to 8px (`stage`: sample frame, interior product hero). Nothing larger. Borders do structural work: 2px keyline around print blocks, 1px thread rules inside them, 1px dashed borders on tickets and badges (a printed tag, not a capsule). Diamonds (an 8px square rotated 45°) separate manifest items.

### Named Rules
**The Keyline Rule.** A print block is outlined by a 2px `keyline` border (in dark theme, `rule-strong` where black would vanish). Inner divisions use 1px `rule`.

## Components

### Buttons
Square, confident, printed.
- **Shape:** gently squared corners (5px), min height 44px.
- **Primary:** Madder Text Red ground with near-white text, 8px 16px padding, 0.875rem weight 700.
- **Hover / Focus:** darkens to `accent-hover`; focus is a 2px `focus` outline at 3px offset (Madder Ink outline inside madder fields).
- **Secondary:** Bleached Surface with a `control` stroke and ink text; hover moves to `paper-3` with an accent border.
- **Field CTA:** on a madder field, the primary action inverts to a resist-white block with indigo text and a 2px keyline, 54px tall, 1.0625rem weight 700, a madder-tinted soft shadow, rising 1px on hover and pressing 1px on active.
- **Secondary on madder:** transparent with a translucent resist stroke; text links on madder are underlined Madder Ink.

### Stamp Blocks (signature)
The script selector under the sample frame is a set of four print blocks in a radiogroup. Each is a 72px-tall square-cornered (2px) block with a 2px keyline, translucent resist face on indigo, a carved lozenge face (the band mask at 28×14px) above its label. Pressing inks it: madder ground, Madder Ink text, the block drops 3px with an inset shadow. Each press re-prints the caption line by line (`stamp-print`, 420ms ease-out, 140ms stagger, from scale 1.06 and 3px blur). Cue-time buttons sit in a quieter row: transparent, 1px translucent border, tabular numerals.

### Origin Ticket
Every sample caption carries a ticket naming script and timing: a 2px-cornered tag with a 1px dashed border, label type with tabular numerals. On the frame it sits on a dark translucent pill ground; in the reading grid it uses `rule-strong`; on madder its border is translucent resist.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 6px (`card`), 8px for the stage frame.
- **Background:** Bleached Surface, or madder for the bilingual swatch row.
- **Shadow Strategy:** flat; see Elevation.
- **Border:** 2px keyline outer, 1px rule inner divisions. Prefer one bordered grid divided by rules over separate floating cards.
- **Internal Padding:** 24px (`lg`).

### Product Hero (interior pages)
Interior product pages print their hero on a madder panel: 2px keyline, 8px radius, `clamp(28px, 4vw, 56px)` padding, the star block at 16% indigo over madder. Its primary button is the field CTA. Under 40rem it bleeds to the screen edge with no radius.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** Bleached Surface, 1px `control` stroke, 5px radius, 44px min height, 8px 12px padding, 1rem text.
- **Focus:** 2px `focus` outline at 3px offset; caret and accent-color are `accent`.
- **Disabled:** `paper-2` ground, `rule` border, muted text, 0.65 opacity.

### Navigation
A sticky three-part bar on resist white (94% paper with 12px backdrop blur), 72px tall, finished underneath by a 10px madder border band (14px keyline band on a resist strip when the page opens on a madder hero). Links are 0.875rem weight 600, 5px-cornered on desktop; the current page is underlined in `accent` at 4px offset. The footer is stage indigo with an 18px Stage Coral border band along its top edge.

### Border Band
An 18px strip of stamped lozenges between two keylines (36×18px tile), printed in `--band-ink`. Used between sections, at the top of the workflow band, framing the close, under the header and over the footer. A 6px dot row (`ajrak-dots`) is its smaller sibling, used on the recommended pricing column.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** let madder own entire bands or blocks: hero, close, interior product hero, the bilingual row, an inked stamp.
- **Do** print every motif through the SVG mask tokens with `--band-ink` so it reprints in both themes.
- **Do** frame print blocks with a 2px `keyline` and divide inside them with 1px `rule`.
- **Do** set Urdu in CaptionUrdu at weight 400, line-height 1.9 to 2.1, letter-spacing normal, with `lang="ur"` and `dir="rtl"`.
- **Do** keep a calm zone of plain madder behind any text set on a printed field.
- **Do** give every sample caption an origin ticket (script, cue time) in tabular numerals.
- **Do** keep hit targets at 44px or more and respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (the stamp animation turns off).

### Don't:
- **Don't** use a black ground with a single neon accent as the hero; the category does that.
- **Don't** warm the light ground toward cream; resist white is cool.
- **Don't** use madder as a small badge fill or tint chip; use `accent` for small red marks.
- **Don't** letter-space, bold or tightly lead Nastaliq.
- **Don't** round anything past 8px or make tags into capsules; tickets and badges are dashed printed tags.
- **Don't** place resist dots or star blocks behind running text.
- **Don't** use uppercase eyebrows or kickers above headings.
