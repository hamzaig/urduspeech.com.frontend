# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Pakistani creators and Urdu speakers preparing captions for reels, interviews, podcasts and longer recordings. Service owners use a separate operations area for payment reviews and private configuration.

## Product Purpose

Turn spoken Urdu into a transcript and timed captions that creators can review, edit and export. Support Urdu script, Roman Urdu, English translation and stacked Urdu–English captions.

## Operating Context

A creator uploads an audio or video file, chooses an output and correction settings, starts transcription after media validation, reviews the words and timing, and downloads TXT, SRT or VTT or renders a captioned MP4. The workspace can preview a local copy of the original recording. The product serves desktop and mobile browsers.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Audio and video use one minute allowance. The free allowance is 10 minutes once. Paid plans are billed in PKR.
- Publish the recorded plan limits, retention and export constraints. Available payment methods come from the workspace API.
- AI can miss names, numbers and timing. The creator reviews the output before publishing.
- Registration requires email verification. The existing product name is UrduSpeech.

## Product Principles

- Treat Urdu script and right-to-left reading as first-class.
- Put the original recording, editable captions and exports in a coherent workflow.
- Use sample captions to demonstrate output choices. Identify demo media as illustrative.
